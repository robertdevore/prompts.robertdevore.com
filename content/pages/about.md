## A Better Home for Useful Prompts

Prompts is Robert DeVore's growing library of structured, reusable prompts for working with AI.

The library began with image-generation prompts and is expanding into writing, business, marketing, coding, and other practical categories. Each entry is designed to be easy to understand, copy, adapt, and use.

## What You'll Find Here

- Complete prompts, not fragments
- Clear examples and supporting notes
- Organized categories for faster browsing
- Copy-ready prompt blocks
