Use this prompt to build a sourced keyword and competitor research brief for a topic. Replace `KEYWORD_HERE` before running it. Access to a current keyword-data platform will make the requested volume and competition figures more reliable.

![An ASCII-grunge magnifying glass examining a keyword and competitor network](/assets/images/ascii-grunge/keyword-competitor-analysis.webp)

## Prompt

```text
KEYWORD_HERE = ``

I am seeking a detailed and thorough keyword analysis for KEYWORD_HERE, encompassing the following elements: Keyword Identification and Analysis: Compile a list of primary keywords pertinent to KEYWORD_HERE.

Include related keywords and long-tail variations. For each keyword, provide monthly search volume (using the most recent data available) and competition level (categorized as low, medium, or high). Highlight keywords that present opportunities due to high search volume and low competition. 

Competitor Content Evaluation: Identify the top five competitors in the KEYWORD_HERE domain. For each competitor, select their most relevant article related to KEYWORD_HERE. Analyze the content (up to 10,000 words per article), focusing on topics covered, depth of coverage, targeted keywords, and strengths and weaknesses in terms of quality, accuracy, and engagement. Identify any gaps or areas where their content is lacking. 

Market Insights: Describe the target audience for KEYWORD_HERE, including demographics, interests, and content preferences. Outline current trends and developments within the KEYWORD_HERE. Pinpoint opportunities for creating content that addresses unmet needs or capitalizes on emerging trends. 

Strategic Content Recommendations: Based on the keyword and competitor analyses, suggest specific content topics or angles that are underserved. Provide guidance on optimizing content for the selected keywords. Offer strategies to differentiate my content from that of competitors, ensuring it stands out in the market. 

Please ensure that all information is current and sourced from reputable platforms. Include citations for all data and information sourced from external websites or platforms. Present the findings in a well-organized report, complete with actionable insights and recommendations for my content strategy.
```

## Best Results

Name the country, language, audience, and time period you care about. Require the model to label unavailable metrics instead of inventing search-volume figures when it cannot access a live keyword-data source.
