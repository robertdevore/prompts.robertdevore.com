Use this prompt when you need an unsparing strategic review of a company, product, or active project. Replace the bracketed placeholder with the subject you want evaluated, and provide any missing background that would make the diagnosis more precise.

![An ASCII-grunge product machine opened for strategic inspection](/assets/images/ascii-grunge/brutally-honest-product-diagnostic.webp)

## Prompt

```text
Give me the full diagnostic on [COMPANY/PRODUCT]. Based on everything you know - from my past prompts, product direction, market position, branding, features, goals, tech stack, and how I operate - give me the brutally honest breakdown. What's working? What's weak? What's unique? What's bullshit I'm telling myself? What's the moat, if any? Where am I wasting time, and where's the leverage? I want the raw truth, not sugar-coated trash. Tear it apart and tell me how to rebuild it stronger. I can take it.
```

## Best Results

Give the model access to the relevant product notes, positioning, customer feedback, roadmap, metrics, and technical context. Ask it to distinguish known evidence from inference so the criticism remains useful rather than theatrical.
