Use this strategic review prompt to examine a business, product, campaign, offer, launch plan, or draft content. It asks two fictional analytical voices to debate the commercial idea, buyer needs, positioning, proof, messaging, channels, and execution constraints before making ranked recommendations.

The voices named **Ogilvy** and **Mather** are fictional roles for the exercise, not quotations or claims about the historical people. One focuses on brand, research, copy, and conversion; the other challenges buyer assumptions, economics, distribution, and feasibility.

## How to Use It

Copy the complete prompt below into your AI assistant. Replace the final placeholder with the material you want reviewed. Include your intended audience, decision, budget, team capacity, and available evidence so the review can address real constraints.

If your assistant can browse or inspect files, provide relevant primary sources. Otherwise, expect a provisional assessment with explicit unknowns. The prompt asks the assistant to distinguish facts, assertions, estimates, and hypotheses rather than inventing traction or proof.

## What the Review Produces

The requested output includes a diagnosis, a debate between the two roles, ranked big ideas, three positioning options, messaging rewrites, objection handling, content and distribution priorities, and specific cuts. It closes with a seven-day plan and a decision register separating what to decide, test, defer, and research.
