Use this AI prompt to write a software release explainer that shows what changed, why it matters, and what readers can do with it. It gives a writing assistant an editorial workflow for investigating a feature, fix, integration, or version before drafting an article for readers who may be new to the project.

## How to Use It

Copy the prompt and fill in the project, repository, and release fields at the end. Point the assistant to a version tag, pull request, commit range, or feature. Add documentation, test results, or release comparisons if the assistant cannot access them directly. You can also specify the audience and where the piece will appear.

## What the Prompt Produces

The assistant recommends a story angle, proposes three headlines, and drafts an explainer and a separate set of conventional release notes. It also suggests visuals and distribution copy. The final fact check pairs technical claims with supporting sources and flags questions the evidence leaves open.
