# Primary guidance reviewed — 2026-10-12

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features — official recommendation: established SEO practices apply to AI features; special AI markup is not required. Maintain crawlable text, links and accurate visible-content-aligned metadata. No citation guarantees.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content — official guidance: original value, accuracy and context matter for generated content. Article labels the three illustrations as AI-generated and distinguishes raster examples from editable vector files.
- https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots — official crawler documentation: search discovery, user-triggered fetching and training are separate roles. Preserve existing policy; user-agent probes cannot establish behavior from verified crawler IPs.
- https://schema.org/BlogPosting — type vocabulary; parsing and correct fields do not prove rich-result eligibility or visibility.
- https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/bing-webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a — official guidelines cover crawlable useful content and prohibit manipulation. The older webmaster-guidelines URL was a client-rendered shell in this research tool.
- https://blogs.bing.com/webmaster/2026/2/Introducing-AI-Performance-in-Bing-Webmaster-Tools-Public-Preview/ — official reporting announcement; account data unavailable here. Citation counts cannot be inferred from local schema checks.

All retrieved 2026-10-12. `llms.txt` and WebMCP remain experimental discovery surfaces, not ranking requirements. The review adds no training-crawler rules, keyword stuffing, invented authority or visibility claims.
