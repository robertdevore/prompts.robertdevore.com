# Current guidance — 2026-09-22

- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features — official guidance: conventional SEO still applies; crawlable text, internal links and visible-content-aligned schema matter. No special AI markup is required; indexing/citations are not guaranteed.
- https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/overview — official recommendation: sitemaps aid discovery, not guaranteed indexing.
- https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots — official crawler documentation: distinguish OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User and GPTBot; preserve owner training policy.
- https://schema.org/BlogPosting — vocabulary reference for the existing article schema; parsing does not establish rich-result eligibility.
- https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a — attempted retrieval; response contained no substantive guidance, so no technical claim relies on it.

All retrieved at audit time. llms.txt and WebMCP remain experimental discovery surfaces, not ranking requirements.
