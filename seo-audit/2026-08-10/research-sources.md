# Research sources

Audit date: 2026-08-10

| Source | Retrieved | Supported conclusion | Classification |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials) | 2026-08-10 | Crawlable links, descriptive text, and helpful people-first content remain core search practices. | Official recommendation |
| [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls) | 2026-08-10 | Redirects and `rel=canonical` are strong canonical signals; sitemap inclusion is weaker and signals should agree. | Official recommendation |
| [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap) | 2026-08-10 | Sitemaps should expose preferred canonical URLs and can be declared in `robots.txt`. | Official recommendation |
| [Google AI features and websites](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features) | 2026-08-10 | AI Overviews/AI Mode require ordinary Search eligibility, not special AI markup; important content should be textual and structured data must match visible content. | Official requirement/recommendation |
| [Google helpful-content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) | 2026-08-10 | Original, useful, clearly authored content should be favored over search-engine-first filler. | Official recommendation |
| [Google site-name guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/site-names) | 2026-08-10 | Homepage `WebSite` data can indicate a preferred site name. | Official recommendation |
| [Google image SEO guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/google-images) | 2026-08-10 | Images need discoverable landing pages, descriptive context, and sound technical delivery. | Official recommendation |
| [Bing Webmaster Guidelines](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/webmaster-guidelines-30fba23a) | 2026-08-10 | Traditional crawl/index/content fundamentals also support Bing/Copilot grounding eligibility. | Official recommendation |
| [Bing IndexNow](https://www.bing.com/webmasters/help/indexnow-0z209wby) | 2026-08-10 | IndexNow can notify participating engines about added, changed, or deleted URLs; it does not guarantee indexing. | Official recommendation; optional |
| [Schema.org](https://schema.org/) | 2026-08-10 | Schema.org is the vocabulary used by the site's JSON-LD; validity is separate from search-feature eligibility. | Official vocabulary |
| [OpenAI crawler documentation](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) | 2026-08-10 | OAI-SearchBot controls ChatGPT search discovery separately from GPTBot training; ChatGPT-User is user-triggered. | Official requirement/recommendation |

`llms.txt` remains an experimental discovery aid in this audit. It is not treated as a Google or Bing ranking requirement.
