# Recommendations and measurement plan

Audit date: 2026-08-10

## Immediate after deployment

- Re-crawl all 19 sitemap URLs and repeat canonical-host, HTTP-to-HTTPS, nested-path, query, robots, sitemap, feed, 404, and crawler-user-agent probes.
- Run production Lighthouse for the same home, article, and category URLs; compare like-for-like with the preserved production baseline.
- Submit the sitemap in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools if not already present. IndexNow is optional and should be adopted only with an owner-managed key and publishing workflow.

## 7-day checks

- Review coverage/crawl errors, sitemap processing, robots access, and any deployment regressions.
- Export query/page impressions, clicks, CTR, country, device, and search appearance from available consoles.
- Run the six controlled AI benchmark questions unchanged across authorized platforms and preserve citations/screenshots.

## 28-, 60-, and 90-day comparisons

- Compare the same query/page cohorts, not sitewide vanity totals alone.
- Compare non-branded discovery, landing-page engagement, conversions, indexed canonical count, crawler errors, field CWV, AI referrals, and AI mention/citation rate.
- Preserve provider, date, locale, device, cited URL, competing domains, and factual accuracy for every AI observation.
- Reassess content opportunities only when query/citation evidence supports them.

## Editorial decisions

- Decide whether empty category pages should remain public placeholders or be held from indexing until original content exists.
- Consider a first-hand guide defining reusable JSON prompts only if observed queries or audience questions justify it.
- Do not add mass FAQs, keyword variants, synthetic reviews, invented dates, or generic AI prose.
